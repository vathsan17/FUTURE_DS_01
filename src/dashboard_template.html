Key takeaways (full dataset)

  • Discounts destroy profit. Orders at 30%+ discount are 14% of order lines but lose about $135K. Orders with no discount earn a 29.5% margin.
  • Furniture is the weak category. It brings $742K sales but only a 2.5% margin. Tables, Bookcases and Supplies lose money overall (Tables: -$17.7K at an average 26% discount).
  • Central region lags (7.9% margin vs 14.9% in West), consistent with its average discount of 24% vs 11% in West. Texas, Ohio, Pennsylvania and Illinois are the biggest loss-making states.
  • Growth is real but seasonal. Sales rose 29% in 2016 and 20% in 2017, and Sep, Nov and Dec bring 43% of annual sales.
  • Loyal customer base. 98% of customers reorder, and the top 10% of customers give 31% of sales.
Source: Sample Superstore dataset (9,994 order lines). Margin = Profit ÷ Sales. Currency assumed USD.
